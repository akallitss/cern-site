About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the detector group at IRFU, CEA Saclay. My work sits at the interface of detector physics, instrumentation and data analysis: understanding how charge is created, amplified and read out in gaseous detectors, and pushing their time and position resolution for future collider and beam-line experiments.

Interests

  • Micro-pattern gaseous detectors (Micromegas, resistive structures)
  • Precise-timing detectors (PICOSEC), fast electronics & waveform analysis
  • Detector simulation: Garfield++, COMSOL/ANSYS FEM, Geant4
  • Test-beam campaigns and detector characterisation
  • Open, reproducible analysis workflows (Python, ROOT, C++)

Research

Micromegas with pillars — FEM & simulation

Field maps and gain studies of pillar-supported amplification gaps; comparison of FEM geometries with measurements. See the simulation report in Notes.

Detector R&D

PICOSEC timing

Cherenkov radiator + photocathode + Micromegas for tens-of-picosecond timing. Prototype characterisation and signal-shape analysis.

Timing

Test-beam analysis

Tracking, efficiency and resolution studies from beam tests; calibration and reconstruction software.
