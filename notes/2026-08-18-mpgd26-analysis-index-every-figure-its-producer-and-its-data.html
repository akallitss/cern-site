MPGD26 analysis index — every figure, its producer and its data

Where each roadmap 5a/5b item lives: figure, the script that makes it, and the product behind it, for the MPGD2026 talk.

Analysis 2026-08-18 MPGD26,P2,BASKET,SPS,analysis,index

Everything the MPGD2026 talk shows is regenerable. This page is the index: for each item of the conference roadmap's §5 wish list — the comparison measurements (5a) and the telescope/physics measurements (5b) — it names the figure, the script that produces it, and the data product underneath.

Three trees, and which is authoritative.

  • Figure producers — P2_basket_analysis/mpgd2026/make_talk_figs.py (cross-campaign), sps_beam_analysis/mpgd26_figs/fig_*.py (beam), cosmic_bench_analysis/22_conference_figures.py (bench), nTof_x17/mpgd26/make_coincidence.py (3D scenes).
  • Stage products — EOS /eos/experiment/ntof/data/x17/p2_sps_july/analysis/, mirrored into the figure workspace by mpgd26_figs/fetch_from_eos.sh. EOS, not the LaCie copy: the LaCie tree predates the condor re-processing.
  • uRWELL-referenced results — TB_July2026_H4/analysis/urw_referenced_efficiency/<run>/urw_p2_efficiency_<run>.csv.

Gathered copies of every talk figure, named by deck slide, live in P2_basket_analysis/conference/figures/; the manifest that puts them there is conference/gather_figures.py.

The 5b headline, in one sentence. With 12 mm pads over a 620 mm lever arm, the three P2 planes localise but do not collimate: the crossing point at the middle plane is reproduced to 3.3 mm = 12 mm/√12, while one pad step corresponds to 19 mrad against a beam that diverges by 1.2 mrad — so 73 % of coincidences fire the same pad on all three planes. Localisation is exactly what the in-situ 2-of-3 tagging scheme needs.

How to rebuild any of it

# beam figures (26 of them), from the EOS copy of the campaign
cd P2_basket_analysis/sps_beam_analysis/mpgd26_figs
./fetch_from_eos.sh [--hv]      # workspace, ~90 MB (+136 MB HV traces)
python3 aggregate.py            # products -> report_data/*.csv
python3 fig_efficiency.py       # and fig_timing / fig_vmm / fig_padmap /
                                # fig_charging / fig_stability / fig_rate /
                                # fig_p2_tracking

# cross-campaign figures
python3 P2_basket_analysis/mpgd2026/make_talk_figs.py -o figs

# 3D scenes
nTof_x17/.venv/bin/python nTof_x17/mpgd26/make_coincidence.py \
    --pairs --gevent 230000195106 --views side,hero

# gather everything the talk uses, with provenance
python3 P2_basket_analysis/conference/gather_figures.py
