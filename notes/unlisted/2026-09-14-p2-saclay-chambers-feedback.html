The two test setups

SPS H4: three P2 chambers (P2_IN, P2_MID, P2_OUT) between two uRWELL reference planes, one real muon track drawn

Saclay cosmic bench: the M3 telescope brackets the P2 chamber under test; the trigger is a top–bottom scintillator coincidence

  • Beam: det1 at P2_MID and det3 at P2_OUT for the whole test; P2_IN held det2, then det4, then det2 again.
  • Bench: one chamber at a time, at the P1 (z = 232 mm) or P2 (z = 712 mm) position.

What each setup actually measures

Bench: det1, 18 h of cosmics, 5 mm sliding window, mesh 415 / drift 615 V. Median 95 % over the whole active area.

Beam: the same chamber at P2_MID, 1.15 M muons, per pad, mesh 450 / drift 700 V. Median 97 % inside the spot.

  • The beam lights only the red patch (170 pads, ~13 % of the pad plane); the bench covers all of it, edges and every pillar included. That is one reason bench numbers are lower.
  • The five large support pillars (red circles) are dead spots in both. Other isolated dark spots on the bench map are single pads, not traced to the bulk.

Cosmic bench: stability

  • Efficiency vs time at fixed HV.
  • det4 charges up: 71 → 92 % over the first ~4 h, then stable.
  • det3 drops 91 → 81 % in 2 h at fixed HV. It later gave no signal at all; the cause was the drift-electrode HV contact.
11

Beam: time resolution

  • Single-chamber time resolution vs drift voltage at mesh 450 V (DREAM).
  • Better with a stronger drift field. At drift 875–900 V: det1 16–17 ns, det3 18 ns, det4* 20 ns.
  • det1 and det3 give the same curve on 26 and 28 Jul: the result is reproducible.
  • The P2 goal of 20 ns is reached.

* The chamber at P2_IN during this 26 Jul run is det4 from the installation timeline; it is not independently confirmed (it could be det2).

Every failure was in the HV connections or the drift frame, which is our assembly. The most useful change for the next chambers is a robust, reproducible HV contact to the mesh and to the drift electrode.

13

Take-aways for the bulk production

  • All four Saclay bulks work. 95–97 % efficiency in the beam for det1, det3 and det4; det2 reached 91 % after its repair, still rising with drift voltage.
  • Bench and beam agree on the same chambers once the gas difference is allowed for.
  • Efficiency is uniform across the whole fan on the bench (det1). The support pillars are visible but cost little: the pillar area is 3.8 %, and the bench misses only ~3 % of tracks with no signal at all.
  • Time resolution of 16–20 ns in the beam, within the P2 goal of 20 ns.
  • Efficiency against the other P2 chambers agrees with the external reference, so the chambers can run P2-style, without extra tracking.

Not covered here: VMM readout (under separate study), and bench mesh scans for det3 (degrading at the time) and det4 (none taken). Numbers are from the August 2026 reprocessing of the bench data and from the July beam analysis.
